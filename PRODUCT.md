# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with visual or motor disabilities, reading or writing difficulties, older and first-time users, speakers of Indian languages, and caregivers helping them complete forms.

## Product Purpose

AwaazForm fills Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, PDFs and Word documents by voice or text. Each answer is read back and filled only after confirmation. The user submits the finished form.

## Brand Personality

Calm, accessible and reassuring. Bolu is the friendly assistant; the waveform is the application mark.

## Anti-references

Preserve the Specify-derived interface documented in CLAUDE.md. Avoid unfamiliar controls, automatic submission, decorative complexity in form workflows, and claims of offline AI.

## Design Principles

- Keep people in control of answers, installation and updates.
- Preserve local data and existing sessions.
- Prefer clear, familiar actions with visible feedback.
- Match the existing interface and translation system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, 44px touch targets, visible keyboard focus, live announcements, high contrast, large text, reduced motion, 13 languages and logical layout for Urdu.
